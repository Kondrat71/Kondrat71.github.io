Story Reels Poster

Story Reels Poster is a personal automation tool. It prepares short vertical videos (Russian-language narration of public stories, with subtitles and background footage) and publishes them to the TikTok account of its owner, on a daily schedule.

The tool is used only by its developer for his own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.
